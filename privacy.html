02What we collect

  • Enquiry data. When you use the enquiry form or write to us: name, e-mail address and, if you choose to provide them, telephone number, country, model of interest and the content of your message.
  • Technical data. The server that hosts this website records standard access logs (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type) for security and to keep the service running.
  • What we do not collect. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising tools, embeds no third-party trackers and builds no profiles of its visitors.

04Who receives it

Your data is handled by authorised Totem staff and by suppliers that act on our instructions as data processors: hosting provider, e-mail provider and form service, if one is connected. Where an enquiry concerns a market served by an authorised partner, we share it with that partner only with your agreement. We never sell personal data.

05Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Data is processed within the European Economic Area wherever possible. If a supplier processes data elsewhere, the transfer takes place only under an adequacy decision or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Confirm once the suppliers are chosen.

06Your rights

You may at any time ask us for access to your data, its correction or erasure, the restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interest (Articles 15 to 22 GDPR). You may withdraw a consent you have given without affecting earlier processing. To exercise a right, write to the address above: we answer within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian supervisory authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it), or with the authority of the country where you live.

07Automated decisions and minors

We make no decisions based solely on automated processing and carry out no profiling. This website is not directed at persons under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

08Links to other websites

Links to Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Google Maps and our press folder open those services in a new tab. From that moment their own privacy policies apply; nothing is loaded from them while you browse this website.

09Changes to this policy

We update this page when our processing changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
