01Paint

Flavours, without limits.

The colour of a Totem must reflect the spirit of its owner. It may be matched to any sample the client brings to the atelier, or chosen from a catalogue of more than forty shades, from the most historic Italian colours to contemporary tones, in pastel, metallic and mica finishes and in double, triple and quadruple layers of exceptional depth.

Turn the car and try a colour. Through the Individual Program, colours are developed together with the client and reserved to a single car.

02Galvanic finishes

Metal speaks one language.

Every brightwork element, from mirrors and badges to door handles, grille shield, hinges, light bezels and the gate plate, is finished in a galvanic tone of the client’s choosing, glossy or satin. Select a finish.

03Carbon Fiber Pack

Exposed carbon, woven for Totem.

Carbon fibre is not hidden at Totem, it is celebrated. The classic twill and two textures drawn exclusively for Totem, the herringbone Totem Design GT and the diamond-patterned Totem Design Wave, each in a glossy or satin lacquer, for the bodywork and the cabin.

04Wheels

Signature wheels.

Two designs, each machined from billet aluminium as an 18-inch monobloc and available in any finish. Nowhere is Totem’s attention to detail more visible.

GTA

A 1960s competition icon, reimagined: a ring of round apertures around a deep, machined centre.

Millerighe

A thousand fine radial lines: timeless modernity, drawing its inspiration from American heritage.

05Leather

06Steering wheels

Three characters, one hand.

Every Totem steering wheel is built around a core machined from aluminium. From there, three characters: the Totem Indy, the Totem Design and the bespoke wheel machined from a single billet, finished in leather, wood, carbon or metal.

Totem Indy

Inspired by the racing wheels of the period, with drilled aluminium spokes.

Totem Design

Drawn by Totem, in leather with contrasting stitching.

Bespoke

Machined from a single billet through the Individual Program.

07Cabin details

Metal, machined from solid.

Switches, instrument bezels, air vents and pedals are machined from billet aluminium, then left natural or finished in the same galvanic palette as the exterior brightwork. Gear knobs in aluminium, wood or Murano glass complete the gesture.

08Audio

Sound, tailored.

A vintage-styled 3.5-inch screen with navigation, DAB+, Bluetooth and USB, and an Audio Development hi-fi system tuned to the cabin, its speakers dressed in the same leathers and weaves as the interior.
